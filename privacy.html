Color Ripple Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

This policy explains how the Color Ripple mobile game (package com.roodeawakening.colorripple), developed by Derek Roode ("we", "us"), collects, uses and shares information. Color Ripple does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email or other contact details.

1. Information collected

Stored only on your device

  • Game progress and history: scores, best score, number of games played, and when you last played.

This data stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Collected by third-party services we use

Service Data Purpose
Google AdMob Advertising ID, IP address (used for approximate location), device and app information, ad interactions Showing ads, measuring ad performance, preventing fraud
Google Analytics for Firebase App instance ID, gameplay and ad events, device type, OS version, approximate location, ad revenue Understanding how the game is played and improving it
Firebase Crashlytics Crash logs, device state at the time of a crash, installation ID Finding and fixing bugs
Firebase Remote Config Installation ID, app version, device language and country Updating game settings without an app update

2. How we use information

  • To run the game and show ads that support it.
  • To measure and improve gameplay, stability and ad performance.
  • Gameplay adjustment: the game adjusts which pieces it deals based on your play history on this device (for example, how many games you've played and your recent scores), whether an ad is ready to show, and an estimate of ad value for your session.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information for money. Information is shared with the Google services listed above, which process it under their own policies. AdMob may use your advertising ID for personalized ads, which some U.S. state laws treat as "sharing" or "selling." You can opt out as described below. We may also disclose information if required by law.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Firebase privacy and security

4. Your choices

  • Ad consent: users in the EEA, UK, Switzerland and certain U.S. states see a consent or opt-out message on first launch. You can change your choice at any time from the game's settings menu.
  • Advertising ID: on Android, go to Settings → Privacy → Ads to delete or reset your advertising ID. Without it, ads won't be personalized.
  • Local data: uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on your device.
  • Requests: to access or delete data linked to your installation, email us. Include the approximate install date; we don't collect your name, so we may need help identifying your data.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including the EEA/UK (GDPR) and U.S. states such as California, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to opt out of targeted advertising or the "sale"/"sharing" of personal information. We won't treat you differently for using these rights. Contact us to make a request.

6. Data retention

Analytics data is kept for the retention period set in Google Analytics (up to 14 months for user-level data). Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days. Advertising data is kept according to Google's policies.

7. Children

Color Ripple is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you think a child has used the app and data was collected, contact us and we'll help delete it.

8. Security

Data sent to Google services is encrypted in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we use reputable providers and collect only what's needed.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If we add new ad networks (for example, AppLovin), they'll be listed here before they're used.
